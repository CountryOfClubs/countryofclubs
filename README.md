# Country of Clubs

**Country of Clubs** is an independent research and comparison resource focused on country clubs across the United States.

Through **CountryOfClubs.com**, we organize and publish information designed to make country clubs easier to research, understand, and compare.

## What We Research

Country of Clubs researches and organizes information about:

* Country club membership costs
* Initiation fees
* Monthly and annual membership dues
* Golf courses and facilities
* Club amenities
* Tennis and pickleball facilities
* Swimming pools and fitness facilities
* Dining and clubhouse facilities
* Private, semi-private, and public access
* Membership policies
* Club locations and operating information

Our goal is to provide a clearer view of what individual clubs offer and what membership may cost.

## About Country of Clubs

Country of Clubs is building a structured resource for researching and comparing country clubs in the United States.

The project focuses on information people commonly want when evaluating a club, including membership pricing, initiation fees, ongoing dues, golf facilities, amenities, dining, access policies, and other club characteristics.
